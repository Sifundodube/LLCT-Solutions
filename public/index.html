For individual employees, the Standard Plan includes unlimited phone and email guidance on workplace rights, personal advice on employment contracts and statutory rights, basic contract reviews, standard grievance-letter drafting, and draft arguments and evidence preparation for disciplinary hearings. The Premium Plan includes everything in Standard, plus direct negotiation assistance with employers, CCMA referrals for conciliation, mediation and arbitration, drafting of arguments and affidavits for CCMA proceedings, full preparation for employees attending con-arb processes at the CCMA, informal representation, and dispute consultation.

Subscription coverage includes personal advice, disciplinary and grievance hearing preparation, dispute consultation, and advice on statutory rights. Formal representation at CCMA, tribunal, or labour court hearings is excluded. Non-subscribers are charged per service.

We also assist employers and organisations with incapacity procedures for ill health, incapacity procedures for poor performance, probation procedures, and retrenchment procedures.

The CCMA (Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration) helps settle work disputes in South Africa. We assist with CCMA referrals, preparation for conciliation, mediation, arbitration and con-arb processes, arguments and affidavits, informal representation, and dispute consultation. Formal representation at CCMA hearings is excluded from the subscription plans.

You need to review your workplace, set targets, take action, and send yearly reports to the Department of Employment and Labour. We can manage this process for you.
